George Russell!

A page about the issues he's faced this year.

George Russell is a driver for Mercedes AMG Petronas Formula 1 team and has been in the sport for 8 years. Before Mercedes, he drove for Williams F1, a perenial backmarker in the time he was there.

George has suffered similar issues the whole year. These issues cost him upwards of 100 points.. These points losses ultimately led to him losing the championship in the year that he was the favorite to win.

Three facts about George's 2026 season

  • A battery failure forced him out of the Canadian Grand Prix, and a collision with Lewis Hamilton ended his race in Belgium. In Hungary, an engine issue sent his car into anti-stall at the start, dropping him from sixth on the grid to the back. He recovered to finish seventh.
  • After Hungary he said he had never had a season like this in his whole career. Later he said he’d only gone six race weekends without an issue, and he asked Mercedes not to write it all off as bad luck.
  • He was the pre-season favourite and won the opening race in Australia, but has won just twice since, while Kimi Antonelli has racked up nine victories. He has conceded the Drivers’ Championship to Antonelli with four rounds still remaining.
